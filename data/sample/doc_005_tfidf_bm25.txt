Term Weighting Strategies: TF-IDF and BM25 in Large Scale Search
Information Processing & Management, 2023
Effective information retrieval hinges upon discerning the statistical significance of terms within individual documents relative to the entire corpus. Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency, known as TF-IDF, weights terms proportionally to their local occurrence while dampening the influence of ubiquitous words across the collection. Standard TF-IDF formulations often suffer from document length bias, favoring excessively lengthy documents. The Okapi BM25 ranking function resolves this limitation by introducing non-linear term frequency saturation and document length normalization parameters, commonly parameterized by k1 and b. When querying domain-specific research corpora, smoothed inverse document frequency prevents zero-division for rare terms and smooths score distributions. Comparative evaluations consistently demonstrate that BM25 provides strong baseline retrieval performance across diverse text genres, serving as an essential first-stage ranker before neural re-ranking models are applied.
